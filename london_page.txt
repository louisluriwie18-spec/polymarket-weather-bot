Whether you are a weather trader, prediction market enthusiast, or meteorology fan, PolyNimbus helps you make data-driven decisions.How Polymarket Weather Markets WorkEach Polymarket weather market asks: "What will be the highest temperature in [city] on [date]?" Traders buy and sell shares on temperature bins (e.g. 66–67°F). Markets resolve using official data from Weather Underground, specifically the highest temperature recorded at the city's airport weather station. Shares in the correct bin pay out $1.00; all others pay $0.Live Data SourcesPolyNimbus combines multiple data sources to give you an edge in weather prediction markets. The METAR panel shows live aviation weather observations — the same airport stations used for market resolution. The order bookdisplays real-time bid/ask spreads via Polymarket's WebSocket CLOB feed. Temperature charts plot Open-Meteo hourly forecasts, METAR history for the day, and ensemble model spread (min/mean/max) so you can see forecast uncertainty.
